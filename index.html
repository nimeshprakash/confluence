Setup 1 detail — Checklists
A+ Trade Rule: a side only becomes a live setup once D1, H4, H1 and M15 are all fully checked (banner turns to "A+ SETUP"). M5 is the optional entry trigger & target box — completing it upgrades the banner to "READY TO ENTER."

What's automatic vs. what isn't: items tagged AUTO are computed every refresh cycle straight from live OHLC data (EMA stack order, RSI(21), CCI(81), EMA pullback proximity, candle patterns, market-structure and overhead-resistance/support heuristics). Market structure and resistance/support are estimated from recent swing pivots — verify visually if a call looks borderline. Enter and Target stay as manual checkboxes with a computed price suggestion next to them, since those are execution decisions, not yes/no conditions. This tool gives you information to make your own trading decision — it isn't financial advice.

Entry & exit pricing on each card: for any of the 30 setups currently signaling BUY or SELL, entry and a probable exit target are derived purely from that setup's own condition and timeframe (shown under its name) — there is no separate market-structure check layered on top. Entry is the current market price at the moment the setup signals. The exit target is the nearest recent swing high (for buys) or swing low (for sells) over the last 20 bars on that timeframe; if price has already moved beyond that extreme, it falls back to a projected move sized off the current swing range. This is a heuristic read of where price may probably reach, not a recommendation — always confirm on the chart before acting on it.

Volatility filter (ATR): every timeframe's ATR(14) is compared against its own 50-bar average. If a setup's primary timeframe is running QUIET (ATR < 0.65× its recent average), that signal is treated as too low-energy to realistically reach its projected target — the card is downgraded to WATCHING and tagged VOL-SUPPRESSED instead of firing a live BUY/SELL. If price is already running EXPANDING and has stretched more than 2.5× ATR away from its own EMA21, the setup still fires but is tagged CHASE RISK as a low-probability-entry warning. The ATR-by-timeframe panel above shows the raw regime read for all five timeframes regardless of which setups are active.

Historical performance tracking: every time a setup freshly flips to BUY or SELL, the portal logs that signal's entry price and target to its own storage. On each refresh it checks open signals against the highs/lows since entry: a signal is marked a win once its target is touched, and if 20 bars pass without that happening it's closed out as a win or loss based on whether price ever moved favorably. Each card shows a running win rate and average % move once it has closed signals — setups with 5+ signals and a win rate under 40% are flagged as historically weak so you can start discounting or ignoring rules that don't actually have edge, rather than treating all 30 setups as equally reliable. Track record data lives only in this portal's storage; use "Reset track record" in Automation to clear it.

Setups 21–24 (intermarket/macro): these are the only setups that look outside gold's own OHLC. DXY Correlation Divergence and VIX Safe-Haven Alignment fetch their symbols on H1 and compare recent moves against gold's; the yield setup does the same for a rate-shock read. All three show NO SIGNAL until you add a symbol in Automation settings, and fail gracefully (no crash) if that symbol isn't on your Twelve Data plan. Setup 24 uses the manual "Next event" time you set — it flags a ±30 min blackout window around it, then a momentum-continuation trigger for 90 minutes after.

Setups 25–30 (additional price action): these diversify signal type rather than adding another EMA/RSI variant — a Turtle-style ATR breakout, a Bollinger squeeze/expansion, classic daily floor pivots, inside-bar/NR7 candle compression, fib-retracement/EMA confluence, and a session open-gap read. All run purely off the gold OHLC you're already fetching, so no extra API calls or setup required.
