A+ Trade Rule: a side only becomes a live setup once D1, H4, H1 and M15 are all fully checked (banner turns to "A+ SETUP"). M5 is the optional entry trigger & target box — completing it upgrades the banner to "READY TO ENTER."

What's automatic vs. what isn't: items tagged AUTO are computed every refresh cycle straight from live OHLC data (EMA stack order, RSI(21), CCI(81), EMA pullback proximity, candle patterns, market-structure and overhead-resistance/support heuristics). Market structure and resistance/support are estimated from recent swing pivots — verify visually if a call looks borderline. Enter and Target stay as manual checkboxes with a computed price suggestion next to them, since those are execution decisions, not yes/no conditions. This tool gives you information to make your own trading decision — it isn't financial advice.

Entry & exit pricing on each card: for any of the 20 setups currently signaling BUY or SELL, entry and a probable exit target are derived from that setup's own timeframe (shown under its name) and verified against market structure on that same timeframe. Entry uses the nearest unmitigated Fair Value Gap (FVG) in the trade's direction if one sits within 2% of price, otherwise the current market price. The exit target is the next opposing liquidity pool (cluster of equal highs/lows) or swing extreme that price is likely to reach in the trade's favor; if no such level clears a reasonable distance from entry, it falls back to a projected move sized off the most recent structural swing. Each card also shows the prevailing structure trend and the most recent BOS (Break of Structure, trend continuation) or CHoCH (Change of Character, first break against the trend) on that setup's timeframe. This is a heuristic read of where price may probably reach, not a recommendation — always confirm structure visually before acting on it.
